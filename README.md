# 📊 Data Analytics: Understanding Career Aspirations of Gen Z

## 📌 Project Overview
This project investigates the career motivations, workplace drivers, financial expectations, and retention factors of Generation Z (Gen Z). By bridging the gap between traditional hiring practices and early-career talent expectations, this study provides actionable insights and strategic recommendations for employers.

The analytical workflow follows a 9-step structure using **SQL**, **Excel**, and **Power BI**, culminating in a case study on the **Royal Bank of Canada (RBC)**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Data Engineering & Analysis:** SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL)
- **Data Modeling & Pivot Summaries:** Microsoft Excel
- **Interactive Dashboards & DAX Measures:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Frameworks:** 5W1H Framework (What, Who, Where, When, Why, How), Win-Win Alignment Matrix

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

- **Top Influencers:** Parents (**1,222**) and real-world changemakers (**783**) exert the strongest influence on Gen Z career choices, far surpassing social media influencers.
- **Leadership Dynamics:** **57%** of respondents prefer managers who set clear expectations and provide supportive feedback over directive target-setting.
- **Tenure Retention:** Structured 1-on-1 mentorship programs paired with predictable salary progression increase 3-year retention likelihood significantly (**1,273 'Yes'** vs. **198 'No'**).
- **Mission Ambiguity:** **27.79%** of participants report working with unclear organizational missions, highlighting a key communication gap for leaders.
- **Work Environment:** Over **70%** of early-career respondents prefer hybrid or remote arrangements with travel flexibility over strict in-office requirements.
