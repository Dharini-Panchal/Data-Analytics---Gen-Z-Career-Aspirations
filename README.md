# 📊 Data Analytics: Understanding Career Aspirations of Gen Z

## 📌 Project Overview
This project investigates the career motivations, workplace drivers, financial expectations, and retention factors of Generation Z (Gen Z). By bridging the gap between traditional hiring practices and early-career talent expectations, this study provides actionable insights and strategic recommendations for employers.

The analytical workflow follows a 9-step structure using **SQL**, **Excel**, and **Power BI**, culminating in a case study on the **Royal Bank of Canada (RBC)**[cite: 1, 30].

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Data Engineering & Analysis:** SQL
- **Data Modeling & Pivot Summaries:** Microsoft Excel
- **Interactive Dashboards & DAX Measures:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Frameworks:** 5W1H Framework, Win-Win Alignment Matrix

---

## 🗺️ Project Roadmap

- **Step 1: Problem Statement** — 5W1H Framework breakdown of Gen Z workplace mismatches[cite: 30].
- **Step 2: Data Collection** — Qualitative & quantitative survey data gathering across demographics and career preferences[cite: 30].
- **Step 3: Data Cleaning & Standardization** — Data sanitization and mapping open-ended responses into standard attributes[cite: 30].
- **Step 4: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** — Demographic profiling, influencer analysis, and retention correlation mining[cite: 30].
- **Step 5: SQL Database Querying** — Relational queries, CTEs, and conditional aggregations[cite: 18, 30].
- **Step 6: Executive Dashboard in Excel** — Dynamic pivot tables, KPI cards, and leadership summaries[cite: 19, 30].
- **Step 7: Functional Dashboard in Power BI** — DAX measures and interactive views (*Manager Aspirations* vs. *Mission Aspirations*)[cite: 21, 22, 23, 30].
- **Step 8: Insights & Recommendations** — Quantitative findings paired with employer action plans and the Win-Win Strategic Matrix[cite: 30].
- **Step 9: Case Study (Royal Bank of Canada - RBC)** — Real-world evaluation of employee sentiment, wellbeing ratings, and frontline vs. executive alignment[cite: 30].

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

- **Top Influencers:** Parents (**1,222**) and real-world changemakers (**783**) exert the strongest influence on Gen Z career choices, far surpassing social media[cite: 16].
- **Leadership Dynamics:** **57%** of respondents prefer managers who set clear expectations and provide supportive feedback[cite: 19].
- **Tenure Retention:** Structured mentorship paired with predictable salary progression significantly increases 3-year retention likelihood[cite: 17].
- **Mission Ambiguity:** **27.79%** of participants report working with unclear organizational missions[cite: 23].
- **Work Environment:** Over **70%** of respondents prefer hybrid or remote arrangements with travel flexibility over strict in-office requirements[cite: 16].
